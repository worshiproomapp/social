Bible verses about healing
Bible verses about healing
By the Worship Room team · November 10, 2026
The Bible describes God as the One who heals: "I am Yahweh who heals you" (Exodus 15:26). Scripture speaks to physical healing, to healing for the brokenhearted (Psalm 147:3), and to the deepest healing of all, through Jesus, "by his wounds we are healed" (Isaiah 53:5). It also invites you to pray for healing with others (James 5:14–15).

Verses for physical healing
“Heal me, O Yahweh, and I will be healed. Save me, and I will be saved; for you are my praise.”
Jeremiah 17:14
A short, honest prayer you can borrow word for word.
“Yahweh will sustain him on his sickbed, and restore him from his bed of illness.”
Psalm 41:3
God's care reaches the sickbed itself. You are not out of His sight there.
“Is any among you sick? Let him call for the elders of the assembly, and let them pray over him, anointing him with oil in the name of the Lord; and the prayer of faith will heal him who is sick, and the Lord will raise him up.”
James 5:14–15
Healing in Scripture is often communal. You aren't meant to pray alone.
“Yahweh my God, I cried to you, and you have healed me.”
Psalm 30:2
A testimony to remember on the days you're still waiting for one of your own.
“Jesus went about all the cities and the villages, teaching in their synagogues and preaching the Good News of the Kingdom, and healing every disease and every sickness among the people.”
Matthew 9:35
Jesus' ministry was full of healing. It shows us God's heart toward the sick.
Verses for emotional healing
“He heals the broken in heart, and binds up their wounds.”
Psalm 147:3
The same God who names the stars (verse 4) bends down to bind up broken hearts.
“Yahweh is near to those who have a broken heart, and saves those who have a crushed spirit.”
Psalm 34:18
When your spirit feels crushed, God doesn't keep His distance. He comes near.
“For I will restore health to you, and I will heal you of your wounds…”
Jeremiah 30:17
Spoken to people who felt like outcasts. God's healing includes being welcomed back.
Verses for healing of the soul
“But he was pierced for our transgressions. He was crushed for our iniquities. The punishment that brought our peace was on him; and by his wounds we are healed.”
Isaiah 53:5
The center of Christian healing: Jesus took on Himself what we couldn't carry.
“Praise Yahweh, my soul, and don’t forget all his benefits, who forgives all your sins, who heals all your diseases, who redeems your life from destruction, who crowns you with loving kindness and tender mercies…”
Psalm 103:2–4
Forgiveness and healing appear side by side. God cares for the whole person.
“But to you who fear my name shall the sun of righteousness arise with healing in its wings.”
Malachi 4:2
A picture of morning after a long night: light that brings healing with it.
When healing hasn't come yet
“He has said to me, ‘My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness.’ Most gladly therefore I will rather glory in my weaknesses, that the power of Christ may rest on me.”
2 Corinthians 12:9
Paul asked three times for relief. God answered with grace that was enough, and power that showed up in weakness.
Not every prayer for healing is answered the way we hope or on our timeline, and Scripture doesn't pretend otherwise. Unanswered prayer isn't proof that your faith is too small. Keep praying, keep asking others to pray with you, and keep going to your doctor. God often works through medicine and the people who practice it.
A prayer for healing
Lord, You are the God who heals. I bring You this body, this heart, and this hurt. Heal me, and I will be healed. Give wisdom to everyone caring for me, strength for each day, and Your peace while I wait. Whatever comes, let me feel Your nearness. Amen.
Frequently asked questions
What does the Bible say about healing?
Scripture presents God as a healer (Exodus 15:26), shows Jesus healing "every disease and every sickness" (Matthew 9:35), invites believers to pray for the sick together (James 5:14–15), and promises healing for the brokenhearted (Psalm 147:3). Ultimately it points to complete healing in the life to come (Revelation 21:4).
What is a good Bible verse to pray for healing?
Jeremiah 17:14 is a simple prayer you can use directly: "Heal me, O Yahweh, and I will be healed." Psalm 103:2–4 and Psalm 147:3 are also widely prayed.
Is it okay to see a doctor if I'm praying for healing?
Yes. Many Christians see medicine as one of the ways God heals. Luke, who wrote a Gospel and Acts, was a physician (Colossians 4:14). Praying and getting care go together.
