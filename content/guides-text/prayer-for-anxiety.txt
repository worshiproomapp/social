A prayer for anxiety
By the Worship Room team · November 10, 2026
Here's a prayer for anxiety you can pray right now: "Father, my mind is racing and my heart is heavy. I'm giving You what I can't carry. Guard my heart and mind with Your peace, and help me take the next small step with You. Amen." Below are more short prayers for specific anxious moments, verses to pray, and a simple way to pray when worry won't let go.

A prayer for anxiety
Father, You know everything I'm carrying, even the worries I can't name. My mind keeps running ahead to what might go wrong. Right now I choose to bring it all to You. Here is what I'm afraid of: [tell Him]. Here is what I'm thankful for: [name one thing]. Thank You that You care for me. Guard my heart and my thoughts with Your peace, the peace I can't explain, and help me live just this day with You. In Jesus' name, amen.
Short prayers for anxious moments
When anxiety hits suddenly
Jesus, I'm overwhelmed. Be close. Slow my breathing, slow my thoughts, and remind me You're here.
Before something you dread
Lord, You're already in that room, that call, that appointment. Go before me, and give me Your peace when I walk in.
When you can't stop overthinking
God, I've replayed this a hundred times. I'm putting it in Your hands and leaving it there. Help me not to pick it back up.
When anxiety comes at night
Father, I can't solve anything tonight, and You never sleep. Watch over everything I'm worried about, and let me rest.
For a friend who is anxious
Lord, my friend is struggling. Surround them with Your peace. Give them people who will sit with them, and help me know how to love them well.
Scripture to pray when you're anxious
Praying Scripture gives you words when your own run out. Read one of these slowly, then turn it into your own sentences.
“In nothing be anxious, but in everything, by prayer and petition with thanksgiving, let your requests be made known to God. And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your thoughts in Christ Jesus.”
Philippians 4:6–7
Pray it: "God, instead of being anxious, I'm bringing You this request, with thanks."
“…casting all your worries on him, because he cares for you.”
1 Peter 5:7
Pray it: "I'm casting this worry on You, because You care for me."
“In the multitude of my thoughts within me, your comforts delight my soul.”
Psalm 94:19
Pray it: "My thoughts are many. Let Your comfort be louder."
“Trust in him at all times, you people. Pour out your heart before him. God is a refuge for us. Selah.”
Psalm 62:8
Pray it: "I'm pouring out my heart to You, because You are my refuge."
A simple way to pray when you're anxious
Name it. Tell God exactly what you're worried about. Be specific. He can handle honesty.
Hand it over. Picture placing each worry in His hands, one at a time.
Give thanks. Name one or two things you're grateful for, even small ones. Philippians 4:6 ties prayer and thanksgiving together.
Rest in a promise. End with a verse like Isaiah 41:10 or John 14:27, and let it be the last word.
Prayer is powerful, and it isn't the only help God gives. If anxiety is affecting your sleep, work, or relationships, talking with a doctor or licensed counselor is a wise and faithful step.
Frequently asked questions
What is a good prayer for anxiety?
A good prayer for anxiety is honest and simple: tell God what you're worried about, hand each worry to Him, thank Him for something, and ask for His peace to guard your heart and mind, as Philippians 4:6–7 describes.
What Bible verse should I pray for anxiety?
Philippians 4:6–7 and 1 Peter 5:7 are the most commonly prayed. Psalm 94:19 and Psalm 62:8 are also good for when your thoughts feel loud.
Does praying help with anxiety?
Many people find that prayer brings real comfort and perspective, and Scripture promises God's peace to those who bring their worries to Him. Prayer works well alongside other care, including support from friends, doctors, and counselors.
