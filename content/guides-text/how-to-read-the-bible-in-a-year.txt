How to read the Bible in a year (and actually finish)
By the Worship Room team · November 3, 2026
To read the whole Bible in a year, read a little over three chapters a day. The Protestant Bible has 66 books and 1,189 chapters, which works out to about 3¼ chapters a day, or roughly 10 to 15 minutes for most readers. Pick a plan, pick a time of day, and when you miss a day, simply pick up with today's reading.

How many chapters a day is it?
1,189 chapters divided by 365 days is about 3.3 chapters a day. Most plans round that into a daily reading of three or four chapters, sometimes mixing a longer Old Testament passage with a shorter New Testament one or a psalm.
How long does it take each day?
For most people, 10 to 15 minutes. Some days are longer (a few chapters of genealogies or law) and some are shorter (a handful of psalms). If you'd like time to reflect or pray afterward, plan on 20 minutes.
Which kind of plan should you choose?
Cover to cover. Genesis to Revelation in order. Simple to follow, though the Old Testament stretches can feel long before you reach the Gospels.
Chronological. Books and passages arranged in the order events happened, so the prophets sit alongside the kings they spoke to. Great for seeing the big story.
Blended. A little Old Testament, a little New Testament, and often a psalm or proverb each day. The variety helps many people keep going.
There's no wrong choice. The best plan is the one you'll still be reading in March.
Tips that help people finish
Attach it to something you already do, like morning coffee, a lunch break, or the last thing before bed.
Start any day of the year. January 1 isn't required. A year from today works just as well.
Read for the story, not for perfect understanding. You'll come back to the hard passages. Keep moving.
Listen when you can't read. An audio Bible on a commute or a walk counts.
Write one line about what you read. A sentence in a journal helps it stick.
What if you fall behind?
Almost everyone does. Don't try to cram a week of chapters into one sitting; that's how plans get abandoned. Just read today's reading and keep going. If you'd rather catch up, add one extra chapter a day until you're back on track. And if a year turns into fourteen months, you've still read the whole Bible.
