Free printable Bible reading plans
By the Worship Room team · November 10, 2026
Here are two free printable Bible reading plans. The 365-day plan takes you through all 1,189 chapters of the Bible in a year, with about two Old Testament chapters and one New Testament chapter or psalm a day. The 30-day starter plan is one page for beginners: one chapter of Mark or John and one psalm a day. Both are free to print, copy, and share.

The 365-day Bible in a year plan
Each day has two readings: an Old Testament reading (about 2 chapters, in order from Genesis to Malachi) and a New Testament reading or psalm (the New Testament in order, with the Psalms woven through the year). Most days take about 15 minutes. Check off each day as you go.
Two tracks keep it fresh. When you're in a long stretch of Leviticus or Chronicles, the New Testament and Psalms keep you encouraged.
All 66 books, every chapter. The plan covers all 1,189 chapters exactly once.
Start any day. You don't have to start on January 1. Write your start date at the top and go.
The 30-day Bible starter plan
If you're new to the Bible, start with Jesus. This one-page plan reads the Gospel of Mark (days 1–16), then John 1–14 (days 17–30), with one psalm each day and a notes column for a verse that stood out or a short prayer.

Tips for sticking with it
Pick a time and a place. Same chair, same time, most days.
Fell behind? Skip to today. Don't let a missed week become a missed year. Circle back to what you missed later, or don't.
Read with a friend. Print two copies and check in once a week.
Pray before you read. Ask God to help you understand and apply what you read.

Frequently asked questions
How many chapters a day to read the Bible in a year?
The Bible has 1,189 chapters, so reading it in a year takes a little over 3 chapters a day (about 3¼), roughly 15 minutes for most readers. This plan gives you about 2 Old Testament chapters plus 1 New Testament chapter or psalm each day.
Can I print and share these reading plans?
Yes. Both PDFs are free to print, copy, and share with your family, small group, or church.
What is the best Bible reading plan for beginners?
For most beginners, a short plan that starts with Jesus works best. The 30-day starter plan reads the Gospel of Mark, then John 1–14, with one psalm a day. When you finish, the 365-day plan is a natural next step.
