Where to start reading the Bible
By the Worship Room team · November 10, 2026
Most people should start reading the Bible with the Gospel of Mark or the Gospel of John, which tell the story of Jesus. After that, read Genesis for the beginning of the story, Psalms for prayer, and Acts and Romans to see how the early church lived and believed. Starting in the middle, with Jesus, makes the rest of the Bible easier to understand.

Why not start at page one?
The Bible is a library of 66 books written over many centuries, not a single novel. Many readers who start at Genesis get stuck in the laws of Leviticus. Starting with Jesus gives you the center of the story first, and everything else connects to Him.
The best books to read first
Mark. The shortest Gospel, fast-paced and action-focused. Most people can read it in about two hours.
John. A more reflective Gospel, written "that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ" (John 20:31).
Genesis. Creation, the fall, and the stories of Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and Joseph. The beginning of God's rescue plan.
Psalms. The Bible's prayer book: praise, lament, and honest emotion. Read one a day.
Acts. What happened after Jesus rose: the birth and spread of the early church.
Romans. Paul's clearest explanation of the gospel and the Christian life.
Proverbs. Practical wisdom for everyday life, in short sayings.
A simple 30-day starter plan
Days 1–16: Mark, one chapter a day.
Days 17–30: John 1–14, one chapter a day.
Every day: one psalm, starting with Psalm 1.

Tips for understanding what you read
Choose a readable translation. Modern translations are easier for most beginners than older ones.
Read a little, slowly. One chapter read carefully beats five skimmed.
Ask three questions: What does this say about God? What does it say about people? What will I do with it today?
Read in context. Read the verses around a famous verse to see what it meant to its first readers.
Read with others. A friend, small group, or church class can help with hard passages.
Pray before you read. Ask God to help you understand and apply it.

Frequently asked questions
What book of the Bible should a beginner read first?
The Gospel of Mark or the Gospel of John is the best place for most beginners to start, because both tell the story of Jesus, who is at the center of the whole Bible. Mark is short and fast-paced; John is more reflective.
Should I read the Bible from beginning to end?
You can, but many beginners find it easier to start with a Gospel, then read Genesis, Psalms, and Acts. Reading front to back works best with a plan that mixes Old and New Testament readings.
How much of the Bible should I read a day?
One chapter a day is a great start. Consistency matters more than amount. At a little over three chapters a day, you can read the whole Bible in a year.
