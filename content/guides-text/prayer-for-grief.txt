A prayer for comfort in grief
By the Worship Room team · November 10, 2026
When grief is too heavy for words, you can pray simply: "God of all comfort, I miss them. Please be near to me, because I can't carry this alone. Amen." Below are prayers for different moments of grief, for anniversaries and holidays, and for a friend who is grieving.

A prayer for comfort after a loss
God of all comfort, my heart is broken. I miss [name] more than I know how to say. Thank You for the gift of their life and for every memory I get to keep. Be near to me in the empty spaces. Hold me on the days I can't get out of bed, and on the days I laugh and then feel guilty for it. Remind me that You catch every tear, and that one day You will wipe them all away. Amen.
Prayers for different moments of grief
In the first days
Lord, I can't think straight. Just be close. Carry me through the next hour, and send the right people to help.
When you're angry
God, I'm angry and confused. I don't understand why this happened. I'm bringing You my anger instead of hiding it. Stay with me in it.
On anniversaries and holidays
Father, today the empty chair feels louder. Help me honor them, let myself feel the loss, and find moments of grace even here.
When others have moved on
Lord, everyone else seems to be moving forward, and I'm not there yet. Thank You that You aren't in a hurry with my grief.
For a friend who is grieving
God, my friend is hurting. Comfort them in ways I can't. Show me when to speak, when to just sit with them, and how to keep showing up.
Scripture to hold on to
“Yahweh is near to those who have a broken heart, and saves those who have a crushed spirit.”
Psalm 34:18
You don't have to reach God. He comes close.
“You count my wanderings. You put my tears into your container. Aren’t they in your book?”
Psalm 56:8
Every tear is counted and kept.
“Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies and God of all comfort, who comforts us in all our affliction…”
2 Corinthians 1:3–4
Comfort is part of God's very name.
“He will wipe away every tear from their eyes. Death will be no more; neither will there be mourning, nor crying, nor pain any more. The first things have passed away.”
Revelation 21:4
The promise at the end of the story.
There's no right way to grieve
Grief isn't a problem to solve or a test of faith to pass. Jesus wept at His friend's grave. Let yourself feel what you feel, lean on others, and give yourself time. If grief feels stuck or starts to swallow your days, a grief counselor or support group can help you carry it.
Frequently asked questions
What is a good prayer for someone grieving?
Ask God to comfort them, to be near to them in their pain, to surround them with caring people, and to give them hope. Second Corinthians 1:3–4 calls God the "God of all comfort," and you can pray that over them by name.
What do you pray after the death of a loved one?
Many people thank God for the person's life, ask for comfort and strength, and pray for the hope of the resurrection. Short prayers are fine. Psalm 34:18 and Revelation 21:4 are good verses to pray.
Is it okay to be angry at God when grieving?
Many psalms express anger, confusion, and sorrow to God. Bringing your honest feelings to Him is part of a real relationship with Him, and He can handle it.
