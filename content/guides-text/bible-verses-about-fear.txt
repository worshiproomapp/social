Bible verses about fear
Bible verses about fear
By the Worship Room team · November 10, 2026
"Don't be afraid" is one of the most repeated commands in the Bible, and it almost always comes with a reason: God is with you. Isaiah 41:10 says, "Don't you be afraid, for I am with you," Psalm 56:3 models trust in the middle of fear, and 2 Timothy 1:7 reminds believers that God didn't give "a spirit of fear, but of power, love, and self-control."

When you're afraid
“When I am afraid, I will put my trust in you. In God, I praise his word. In God, I put my trust. I will not be afraid. What can flesh do to me?”
Psalm 56:3–4
David wrote this while captured by enemies. Fear came first; trust answered it.
“I sought Yahweh, and he answered me, and delivered me from all my fears.”
Psalm 34:4
God doesn't just calm fears. He delivers from them.
“Yahweh is my light and my salvation. Whom shall I fear? Yahweh is the strength of my life. Of whom shall I be afraid?”
Psalm 27:1
Two questions that answer themselves once you know who God is.
“Yahweh is on my side. I will not be afraid. What can man do to me?”
Psalm 118:6
Perspective: the One on your side is bigger than whatever you're facing.
God is with you
“Don’t you be afraid, for I am with you. Don’t be dismayed, for I am your God. I will strengthen you. Yes, I will help you. Yes, I will uphold you with the right hand of my righteousness.”
Isaiah 41:10
YouVersion's most downloaded verse of 2025, and no wonder: every line is a promise.
“Yahweh himself is who goes before you. He will be with you. He will not fail you nor forsake you. Don’t be afraid. Don’t be discouraged.”
Deuteronomy 31:8
He goes before you. Wherever you're headed, God is already there.
“Haven’t I commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Don’t be afraid. Don’t be dismayed, for Yahweh your God is with you wherever you go.”
Joshua 1:9
Courage isn't the absence of fear. It's moving forward because God goes with you.
“But now Yahweh who created you, Jacob, and he who formed you, Israel, says: ‘Don’t be afraid, for I have redeemed you. I have called you by your name. You are mine. When you pass through the waters, I will be with you, and through the rivers, they will not overflow you. When you walk through the fire, you will not be burned, and flame will not scorch you.”
Isaiah 43:1–2
"You are mine." Waters and fire still come, but they don't get the final say.
Fear in the dark and in the storm
“He will cover you with his feathers. Under his wings you will take refuge. His faithfulness is your shield and rampart. You shall not be afraid of the terror by night, nor of the arrow that flies by day…”
Psalm 91:4–5
Feathers and wings: one of Scripture's most tender pictures of protection.
“Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for you are with me. Your rod and your staff, they comfort me.”
Psalm 23:4
The valley is real. So is the Shepherd.
“He awoke and rebuked the wind, and said to the sea, ‘Peace! Be still!’ The wind ceased and there was a great calm. He said to them, ‘Why are you so afraid? How is it that you have no faith?’”
Mark 4:39–40
The storm obeyed Him. The disciples' fear didn't cancel His care.
Love that drives out fear
“There is no fear in love; but perfect love casts out fear…”
1 John 4:18
Fear shrinks as you understand how completely you are loved.
“Aren’t two sparrows sold for an assarion coin? Not one of them falls to the ground apart from your Father’s will. But the very hairs of your head are all numbered. Therefore don’t be afraid. You are of more value than many sparrows.”
Matthew 10:29–31
If God tracks sparrows and counts hairs, you are not overlooked.
“For God didn’t give us a spirit of fear, but of power, love, and self-control.”
2 Timothy 1:7
Fear is a feeling, not your identity. God's Spirit gives power, love, and a sound mind.
A prayer when you're afraid
Lord, I'm afraid. You already know that, so I won't pretend. You said, "I am with you," so be with me now. Steady my heart, quiet my thoughts, and give me courage for the next thing in front of me. When I am afraid, I will put my trust in You. Amen.
Frequently asked questions
How many times does the Bible say "Don't be afraid"?
Versions of "Don't be afraid" appear many dozens of times, and the exact count varies by translation and by which phrases you include. More important than the number is the pattern: the command is almost always followed by a reason, usually God's presence.
What is the best Bible verse for fear?
Isaiah 41:10 is the most widely used: "Don't you be afraid, for I am with you." Psalm 56:3 and 2 Timothy 1:7 are also favorites.
Is fear a sin?
Feeling afraid is human, and Scripture is full of faithful people who were afraid. The invitation is to bring fear to God and trust Him in it (Psalm 56:3), not to pretend it isn't there.
