What to Pray When You Can't Sleep
What to pray when you can't sleep
By the Worship Room team · November 3, 2026
When you can't sleep, pray short and simple. Tell God what's keeping you awake, hand it to Him one worry at a time, and rest in the truth that He keeps watch while you sleep. A slow breath prayer, a single verse repeated quietly, or a few honest sentences are often enough.

Short prayers for sleepless nights
When your mind won't stop racing
Lord, my thoughts are running in circles. I can't solve any of this tonight, and I don't have to. I'm setting each one down in front of You. Quiet my mind and let me rest.
When you're anxious about tomorrow
Father, tomorrow is already in Your hands. Whatever it holds, You'll be there before I am. Give me sleep tonight and strength for the morning.
When you're grieving
God, the night feels long and the hurt feels close. Stay near me. I don't need answers right now, just Your presence.
After a hard day
Jesus, today was heavy. Forgive me where I fell short, heal what was hurt, and let this day be finished. I'm done carrying it.
When you're just exhausted
Lord, I'm tired. Thank You that You never are. Watch over me and everyone I love while I sleep.
A simple breath prayer
A breath prayer is a short prayer you pray in rhythm with your breathing. One of the oldest is the Jesus Prayer. Breathe in slowly: "Lord Jesus Christ," and breathe out: "have mercy on me." Repeat it gently for a few minutes. When your mind wanders, simply come back to the words.

Gentle ways to quiet your mind before bed
Write your worries down before you turn off the light, then leave them on the page.
Dim your screen and keep anything you listen to slow and quiet.
Try soft background sound like rain or a fan, which helps many people drift off.
Choose one verse and repeat it slowly instead of scrolling.
If sleepless nights go on for weeks or start affecting your days, it's worth talking with a doctor. Trouble sleeping can have causes that are easy to help.
