How to start a prayer journal
By the Worship Room team · November 3, 2026
A prayer journal is simply a place where you write your prayers to God. To start, choose a notebook or an app, set aside five quiet minutes, write today's date, and tell God honestly what's on your heart. A simple method like ACTS or SOAP can give you structure on the days you don't know what to say.

Why keep a prayer journal?
It slows your thoughts down. Writing a prayer keeps your mind from wandering the way it can when you pray silently.
It helps you be honest. Many people find they can write what they can't yet say out loud.
It helps you remember. Months later you can look back and see prayers God answered, and how far He's carried you.
“Trust in him at all times, you people. Pour out your heart before him. God is a refuge for us.”
Psalm 62:8
How to start, step by step
Pick your journal. A paper notebook, a notes app, or a journaling app. Whatever you'll actually open.
Pick a small, regular time. Five minutes in the morning or before bed is plenty to begin.
Date every entry. It turns your journal into a record you can look back on.
Start with one honest sentence. "God, today I feel…" is enough.
Keep a running list of requests, and mark the ones that are answered.
Read back once a month. This is where gratitude grows.
Two simple methods
ACTS
Adoration: praise God for who He is.
Confession: name what you need forgiveness for.
Thanksgiving: thank Him for specific things from today.
Supplication: bring your requests, for yourself and for others.
SOAP
Scripture: write down a verse or short passage you read today.
Observation: what does it say? What stands out to you?
Application: what might it mean for your life this week?
Prayer: write a prayer in response.
Prompts for when you're stuck
What's weighing on me most right now?
Where did I notice God today?
What am I grateful for, even in a hard season?
What am I afraid of, and what do I want to ask God about it?
Who needs prayer from me this week?
What verse has stuck with me lately, and why?
What would I say to God if I knew He was listening? (He is.)
Keeping it private
A prayer journal is for you and God. If you journal in an app, choose one where your entries stay private and aren't shared or shown to anyone.
