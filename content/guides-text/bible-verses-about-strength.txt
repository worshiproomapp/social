Bible verses about strength
By the Worship Room team · November 10, 2026
The Bible's message about strength is surprising: God gives strength to the weary, and His power is made perfect in weakness. Isaiah 40:29–31 promises that those who wait for the Lord will renew their strength, Philippians 4:13 says "I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me," and Psalm 46:1 calls God "our refuge and strength."

When you're running on empty
“He gives power to the weak. He increases the strength of him who has no might. Even the youths faint and get weary, and the young men utterly fall; but those who wait for Yahweh will renew their strength. They will mount up with wings like eagles. They will run, and not be weary. They will walk, and not faint.”
Isaiah 40:29–31
Even the young and strong run out. The promise isn't that you'll never be tired, but that God renews those who wait for Him.
“My flesh and my heart fails, but God is the strength of my heart and my portion forever.”
Psalm 73:26
When body and heart both fail, God is still "the strength of my heart."
“He has said to me, ‘My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness.’ Most gladly therefore I will rather glory in my weaknesses, that the power of Christ may rest on me. Therefore I take pleasure in weaknesses, in injuries, in necessities, in persecutions, and in distresses, for Christ’s sake. For when I am weak, then am I strong.”
2 Corinthians 12:9–10
God's answer to weakness isn't always removing it. Sometimes it's showing His power through it.
Strength to keep going
“I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.”
Philippians 4:13
Paul wrote this about contentment in hunger and in plenty (verses 11–12). It's strength to endure every circumstance, not a promise of unlimited achievement.
“Yahweh is my strength and my shield. My heart has trusted in him, and I am helped. Therefore my heart greatly rejoices. With my song I will thank him.”
Psalm 28:7
Strength and song together. Trust turns into thanks.
“…the joy of Yahweh is your strength.”
Nehemiah 8:10
Said to people weeping over their failures. God's joy, not your performance, is what holds you up.
“Yah is my strength and song. He has become my salvation. This is my God, and I will praise him; my father’s God, and I will exalt him.”
Exodus 15:2
The song Israel sang on the far side of the sea. Strength you've seen God provide becomes a song you can sing again.
Strength and courage for what's ahead
“Haven’t I commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Don’t be afraid. Don’t be dismayed, for Yahweh your God is with you wherever you go.”
Joshua 1:9
Courage here rests on one fact: God goes with you "wherever you go."
“Be strong and courageous. Don’t be afraid or scared of them, for Yahweh your God himself is who goes with you. He will not fail you nor forsake you.”
Deuteronomy 31:6
Spoken to a people about to face giants. He will not fail you nor forsake you.
“Finally, be strong in the Lord and in the strength of his might.”
Ephesians 6:10
Strength "in the Lord." It's borrowed strength, and it never runs dry.
“Don’t you be afraid, for I am with you. Don’t be dismayed, for I am your God. I will strengthen you. Yes, I will help you. Yes, I will uphold you with the right hand of my righteousness.”
Isaiah 41:10
"I will strengthen you" is personal. It's addressed to you, not to the strong.
“God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.”
Psalm 46:1
Refuge and strength: a place to hide and the power to stand.
A prayer for strength
Lord, I don't have much left today. You give power to the weak, so I'm coming to You weak. Renew my strength for the next hour, the next conversation, the next step. Let Your power show up where I'm running out. Amen.
Finding strength day by day
Start small. One verse in the morning is enough. Isaiah 40:31 or Psalm 46:1 fit on a sticky note.
Rest on purpose. Even Jesus withdrew to quiet places to pray (Luke 5:16). Rest is part of strength, not the opposite of it.
Let others carry you. Ask someone to pray for you this week. Strength in Scripture is often shared.
Frequently asked questions
What does the Bible say about strength?
The Bible teaches that real strength comes from God, especially when we're weak. God "gives power to the weak" (Isaiah 40:29), His power is "made perfect in weakness" (2 Corinthians 12:9), and believers are told to "be strong in the Lord" (Ephesians 6:10).
What does Philippians 4:13 really mean?
In context, Paul is describing contentment in every situation, whether hungry or well fed (Philippians 4:11–12). The verse means Christ gives the strength to endure and be content in any circumstance, rather than a promise that we can achieve anything we set our minds to.
What is a short Bible verse about strength?
Psalm 46:1 ("God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble") and Nehemiah 8:10 ("the joy of Yahweh is your strength") are short and easy to memorize.
