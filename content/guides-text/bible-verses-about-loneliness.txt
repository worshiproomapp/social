Bible verses about loneliness
Bible verses about loneliness
By the Worship Room team · November 10, 2026
The Bible promises that you are never truly alone. Jesus said, "I am with you always" (Matthew 28:20), Hebrews 13:5 says God will never leave you or forsake you, and Psalm 68:6 says "God sets the lonely in families." Scripture also shows that loneliness is a real human ache. The psalmists felt it, and so did Jesus.

God hasn't left you
“‘I will in no way leave you, neither will I in any way forsake you.’”
Hebrews 13:5
The Greek piles up negatives for emphasis. Never, ever, ever.
“Behold, I am with you always, even to the end of the age.”
Matthew 28:20
Jesus' last words in Matthew are a promise of presence.
“Yahweh himself is who goes before you. He will be with you. He will not fail you nor forsake you. Don’t be afraid. Don’t be discouraged.”
Deuteronomy 31:8
God goes before you, so wherever you walk next, He's already there.
“I will not leave you orphans. I will come to you.”
John 14:18
Orphans are the picture of being left. Jesus promises the opposite.
You are known
“Where could I go from your Spirit? Or where could I flee from your presence? If I ascend up into heaven, you are there. If I make my bed in Sheol, behold, you are there! If I take the wings of the dawn, and settle in the uttermost parts of the sea, even there your hand will lead me, and your right hand will hold me.”
Psalm 139:7–10
There's nowhere you can go that's beyond God's reach, even the bottom of the sea.
“Can a woman forget her nursing child, that she should not have compassion on the son of her womb? Yes, these may forget, yet I will not forget you! Behold, I have engraved you on the palms of my hands. Your walls are continually before me.”
Isaiah 49:15–16
Even if the people closest to you forget, God says, "I will not forget you."
“When my father and my mother forsake me, then Yahweh will take me up.”
Psalm 27:10
For anyone who has felt abandoned by family: God takes you up.
When loneliness hurts
“Turn to me, and have mercy on me, for I am desolate and afflicted.”
Psalm 25:16
An honest prayer from a lonely heart. You can pray it too.
“Yahweh is near to those who have a broken heart, and saves those who have a crushed spirit.”
Psalm 34:18
God draws closest when you feel most broken.
“He heals the broken in heart, and binds up their wounds.”
Psalm 147:3
Loneliness can wound. God binds up wounds.
God made you for connection
“God sets the lonely in families.”
Psalm 68:6
God's answer to loneliness often comes through people.
“Two are better than one, because they have a good reward for their labor. For if they fall, the one will lift up his fellow; but woe to him who is alone when he falls, and doesn’t have another to lift him up.”
Ecclesiastes 4:9–10
Two really are better than one. Reaching out isn't weakness; it's how we were made.
“But he withdrew himself into the desert and prayed.”
Luke 5:16
Being alone with God is different from being lonely. Jesus sought out solitude to pray.
A prayer when you feel alone
God, I feel alone today. Thank You that You see me even when no one else seems to. Be close to me right now. Open a door to real friendship, give me courage to reach out, and help me notice someone else who might be lonely too. Amen.
Small steps toward connection
Reach out to one person this week. A short message counts.
Join something small. A Bible study, a volunteer team, or a church small group is often easier than a big crowd.
Pray for others by name. Praying for people builds a quiet kind of connection, even before you meet.
Talk to someone. If loneliness has turned into a heaviness that won't lift, a pastor or counselor can help.
Frequently asked questions
What does the Bible say about loneliness?
The Bible acknowledges loneliness as a real pain (Psalm 25:16) while promising God's constant presence (Hebrews 13:5, Matthew 28:20). It also shows that God often answers loneliness through people: "God sets the lonely in families" (Psalm 68:6).
Did Jesus feel lonely?
Jesus experienced deep loneliness. His closest friends fell asleep while He prayed in Gethsemane, and many deserted Him at His arrest. He understands the ache from the inside.
What is a Bible verse for feeling alone?
Deuteronomy 31:8 ("He will be with you. He will not fail you nor forsake you") and Isaiah 49:15–16 ("I will not forget you") are two of the most comforting.
