Bible verses about God's love
Bible verses about God's love
By the Worship Room team · November 10, 2026
The Bible's clearest statement about God is "God is love" (1 John 4:16). Scripture shows that love most fully in Jesus: "For God so loved the world, that he gave his only born Son" (John 3:16). God's love is everlasting (Jeremiah 31:3), comes before we deserve it (Romans 5:8), and nothing can separate us from it (Romans 8:38–39).

God's love shown in Jesus
“For God so loved the world, that he gave his only born Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish, but have eternal life.”
John 3:16
The most famous verse in the Bible is a love verse.
“But God commends his own love toward us, in that while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.”
Romans 5:8
God didn't wait for you to get your life together. He loved you first.
“By this God’s love was revealed in us, that God has sent his only born Son into the world that we might live through him. In this is love, not that we loved God, but that he loved us, and sent his Son as the atoning sacrifice for our sins.”
1 John 4:9–10
Love is defined by what God did, not by how well we love Him back.
“I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave himself up for me.”
Galatians 2:20
Paul makes it personal: "who loved me." You can say that too.
A love that never ends
“Yes, I have loved you with an everlasting love. Therefore I have drawn you with loving kindness.”
Jeremiah 31:3
Everlasting means it doesn't run out, wear thin, or expire.
“Give thanks to Yahweh, for he is good, for his loving kindness endures forever.”
Psalm 136:1
Psalm 136 repeats "his loving kindness endures forever" twenty-six times. Some truths need repeating.
“For the mountains may depart, and the hills be removed, but my loving kindness will not depart from you, and my covenant of peace will not be removed,’ says Yahweh who has mercy on you.”
Isaiah 54:10
Mountains can move. God's love won't.
“It is because of Yahweh’s loving kindnesses that we are not consumed, because his mercies don’t fail. They are new every morning. Great is your faithfulness.”
Lamentations 3:22–23
Fresh every morning, no matter how yesterday went.
Nothing can separate you
“For I am persuaded that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor things present, nor things to come, nor powers, nor height, nor depth, nor any other created thing will be able to separate us from God’s love which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.”
Romans 8:38–39
Paul lists everything he can think of, and none of it is strong enough.
“For as the heavens are high above the earth, so great is his loving kindness toward those who fear him. As far as the east is from the west, so far has he removed our transgressions from us.”
Psalm 103:11–12
As high as the sky, and your failures as far away as east is from west.
Loved as God's child
“See how great a love the Father has given to us, that we should be called children of God! For this cause the world doesn’t know us, because it didn’t know him.”
1 John 3:1
Not just tolerated, not just forgiven: called children.
“Yahweh, your God, is among you, a mighty one who will save. He will rejoice over you with joy. He will calm you in his love. He will rejoice over you with singing.”
Zephaniah 3:17
God rejoices over you with singing. Let that sink in.
“…that Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith, to the end that you, being rooted and grounded in love, may be strengthened to comprehend with all the saints what is the width and length and height and depth, and to know Christ’s love which surpasses knowledge, that you may be filled with all the fullness of God.”
Ephesians 3:17–19
Paul prays you'd grasp a love so big it "surpasses knowledge."
“We know and have believed the love which God has for us. God is love, and he who remains in love remains in God, and God remains in him.”
1 John 4:16
Knowing God's love starts with believing it's true for you.
A prayer to receive God's love
Father, it's hard for me to believe You love me like this. Help me stop trying to earn it. Let me rest in the love You showed in Jesus, the love that was there before I knew You and will be there forever. Root me in it, and let it spill over to the people around me. Amen.
Frequently asked questions
What does the Bible say about God's love?
The Bible says "God is love" (1 John 4:16), that He showed His love by sending Jesus (John 3:16, Romans 5:8), that His love is everlasting (Jeremiah 31:3), and that nothing can separate us from it (Romans 8:38–39).
Is God's love unconditional?
Scripture teaches that God loves us before we do anything to deserve it: "while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us" (Romans 5:8). His love is not earned by good behavior, though it does invite a response of faith and love in return.
What is the best Bible verse about God's love for me?
John 3:16 is the best known. Romans 8:38–39 is often the most comforting, and Zephaniah 3:17 is a beautiful picture of God rejoicing over you.
