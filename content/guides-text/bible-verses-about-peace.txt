Bible verses about peace
By the Worship Room team · November 10, 2026
The Bible describes peace as more than calm circumstances. It's a gift from Jesus ("My peace I give to you," John 14:27), a peace that "surpasses all understanding" and guards your heart (Philippians 4:7), and peace with God Himself through faith (Romans 5:1).

Peace that Jesus gives
“Peace I leave with you. My peace I give to you; not as the world gives, I give to you. Don’t let your heart be troubled, neither let it be fearful.”
John 14:27
Jesus said this the night before the cross. His peace held even then.
“I have told you these things, that in me you may have peace. In the world you have trouble; but cheer up! I have overcome the world.”
John 16:33
Jesus doesn't promise a trouble-free life. He promises peace in Him while trouble is still real.
“For a child is born to us. A son is given to us; and the government will be on his shoulders. His name will be called Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace.”
Isaiah 9:6
One of His names is Prince of Peace. Peace isn't just something He gives; it's who He is.
Peace that guards your mind
“And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your thoughts in Christ Jesus.”
Philippians 4:7
"Guard" is a military word. God's peace stands watch over your heart and thoughts.
“You will keep whoever’s mind is steadfast in perfect peace, because he trusts in you.”
Isaiah 26:3
Perfect peace, literally "peace, peace" in Hebrew, for a mind that stays on God.
“And let the peace of God rule in your hearts, to which also you were called in one body, and be thankful.”
Colossians 3:15
Let peace be the deciding voice in your heart, and let thankfulness follow.
“For the mind of the flesh is death, but the mind of the Spirit is life and peace…”
Romans 8:6
Where your mind is set shapes how your soul feels.
Peace with God
“Being therefore justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ…”
Romans 5:1
The deepest peace in Scripture: no longer at odds with God, because of Jesus.
Peace to rest in
“In peace I will both lay myself down and sleep, for you alone, Yahweh, make me live in safety.”
Psalm 4:8
A bedtime verse. Sleep becomes an act of trust.
“Be still, and know that I am God.”
Psalm 46:10
Stillness isn't doing nothing. It's remembering who is God, and who isn't.
“Yahweh will give strength to his people. Yahweh will bless his people with peace.”
Psalm 29:11
Strength and peace come from the same hands.
“Now may the Lord of peace himself give you peace at all times in all ways. The Lord be with you all.”
2 Thessalonians 3:16
"At all times in all ways" covers this exact moment.
A blessing of peace
This ancient blessing, given to Aaron to speak over Israel, is still prayed in churches every week. Read it slowly over yourself or someone you love:
“Yahweh bless you, and keep you. Yahweh make his face to shine on you, and be gracious to you. Yahweh lift up his face toward you, and give you peace.”
Numbers 6:24–26
A prayer for peace
Jesus, You said Your peace is different from the world's. I need that kind today. Quiet the noise in my mind, settle my heart, and let Your peace guard me like a sentry at the door. Help me be still long enough to remember that You are God. Amen.
Frequently asked questions
What does the Bible say about peace?
Scripture speaks of peace with God through faith in Jesus (Romans 5:1), the peace of God that guards our hearts and minds (Philippians 4:7), and peace that Jesus gives even in a troubled world (John 14:27, John 16:33).
What is the peace that surpasses all understanding?
It's the phrase from Philippians 4:7 describing a peace from God that doesn't depend on circumstances or on having all the answers. Paul connects it to bringing every worry to God in prayer with thanksgiving (Philippians 4:6).
What is a Bible verse for peace of mind?
Isaiah 26:3 is a favorite: "You will keep whoever's mind is steadfast in perfect peace, because he trusts in you." Philippians 4:6–7 and John 14:27 are also widely used.
