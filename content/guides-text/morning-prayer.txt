A morning prayer to start your day
By the Worship Room team · November 10, 2026
A morning prayer can be short: "Lord, this is the day You have made. I give it to You. Guide my steps, guard my words, and help me notice You today. Amen." Below you'll find a fuller morning prayer, short prayers for different kinds of mornings, verses to pray, and a simple five-minute routine.

A morning prayer
Good morning, Lord. Thank You for waking me up and for mercies that are new again today. Before the noise starts, I give You this day: my plans, my work, my conversations, and the things I'm worried about. Guide my steps. Give me patience with the people I'll meet, and courage for whatever is hard. Help me notice You in ordinary moments, and let my words and actions point back to You. Amen.
Short morning prayers
For a busy day
Lord, I don't have much time, but I have this minute. Be with me in every task, and keep me calm when the day speeds up.
For a hard day ahead
Father, I know today will be difficult. Go before me. Give me strength for each hour, not all of them at once.
For gratitude
Thank You for breath, for this morning, and for the people I love. Let thankfulness set the tone for today.
For your family
God, watch over my family today. Protect them, guide them, and let our home be full of kindness.
For work or school
Lord, help me work with integrity and care. Give me wisdom for my decisions and kindness for the people around me.
Verses to pray in the morning
“Yahweh, in the morning you will hear my voice. In the morning I will lay my requests before you, and will watch expectantly.”
Psalm 5:3
Morning prayer is as old as the Psalms: lay your requests before God and watch expectantly.
“It is because of Yahweh’s loving kindnesses that we are not consumed, because his mercies don’t fail. They are new every morning. Great is your faithfulness.”
Lamentations 3:22–23
Yesterday's mistakes don't carry over. Mercy starts fresh.
“Cause me to hear your loving kindness in the morning, for I trust in you. Cause me to know the way in which I should walk, for I lift up my soul to you.”
Psalm 143:8
Ask to hear God's love first thing, and to know which way to walk.
“This is the day that Yahweh has made. We will rejoice and be glad in it!”
Psalm 118:24
A simple verse to say out loud as you get up.
“Satisfy us in the morning with your loving kindness, that we may rejoice and be glad all our days.”
Psalm 90:14
Let God's love set the tone for the rest of the day.
A simple five-minute morning routine
One minute of quiet. Before your phone, sit still and breathe. Jesus Himself rose early to pray in a quiet place (Mark 1:35).
Read one short passage. A psalm, a few verses from a Gospel, or today's devotional.
Pray through your day. Walk through your calendar with God, one meeting or task at a time.
Name one thing you're thankful for. Write it down if you can.
Take one verse with you. Pick a line to come back to at lunch.
“Early in the morning, while it was still dark, he rose up and went out, and departed into a deserted place, and prayed there.”
Mark 1:35
If Jesus needed quiet time with the Father before a busy day, so do we.
Frequently asked questions
What is a good morning prayer?
A good morning prayer thanks God for the new day, offers Him your plans, and asks for guidance and strength. Psalm 5:3 and Psalm 143:8 are morning prayers straight from Scripture.
What is a short morning prayer to start the day?
Try: "Lord, this is the day You have made. I give it to You. Guide my steps, guard my words, and help me notice You today. Amen." Psalm 118:24 also works as a one-line morning prayer.
Why pray in the morning?
Praying in the morning helps set the direction of your day before distractions arrive. The psalmists prayed in the morning (Psalm 5:3), and Jesus rose early to pray (Mark 1:35). That said, God hears prayer at any hour.
