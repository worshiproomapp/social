A prayer for healing
By the Worship Room team · November 10, 2026
A prayer for healing can be as simple as Jeremiah 17:14: "Heal me, O Yahweh, and I will be healed." Below are prayers for healing for yourself, for someone you love, before surgery, and for a broken heart, along with Scripture to pray and encouragement for the waiting.

A prayer for healing for yourself
Lord, You are the God who heals. You know this body better than any doctor, and You know how tired and afraid I am. I'm asking You to heal me. Touch every place that hurts. Give wisdom to the people caring for me and strength for each day. And whatever happens, let me feel You near. I trust You. In Jesus' name, amen.
A prayer for healing for someone you love
Father, I'm bringing [name] to You. You love them even more than I do. Please heal them. Ease their pain, calm their fears, and surround them with Your presence through every test and treatment. Guide their doctors and nurses. Give our family patience and hope while we wait. Amen.
A prayer before surgery
Lord, I'm placing this surgery in Your hands. Steady the hands of every surgeon and nurse. Keep me safe, give me peace as I go under, and be with me as I wake. Watch over the people waiting for me. Amen.
A prayer for emotional healing
God, some of my wounds don't show. You heal the brokenhearted and bind up their wounds, so I'm asking You to heal mine. Where there's bitterness, bring forgiveness. Where there's shame, bring Your kindness. Where there's grief, bring comfort. Make me whole again, one day at a time. Amen.
Scripture to pray for healing
“Heal me, O Yahweh, and I will be healed. Save me, and I will be saved; for you are my praise.”
Jeremiah 17:14
A ready-made prayer. You can pray it word for word.
“Praise Yahweh, my soul, and don’t forget all his benefits, who forgives all your sins, who heals all your diseases, who redeems your life from destruction, who crowns you with loving kindness and tender mercies…”
Psalm 103:2–4
Remind your soul of who God is, especially when healing feels slow.
“He heals the broken in heart, and binds up their wounds.”
Psalm 147:3
For the heart as much as the body.
“Is any among you sick? Let him call for the elders of the assembly, and let them pray over him, anointing him with oil in the name of the Lord; and the prayer of faith will heal him who is sick, and the Lord will raise him up.”
James 5:14–15
An invitation to ask your church to pray with you.
When healing takes time
Sometimes healing comes quickly. Often it comes slowly, through treatment and recovery. Sometimes it doesn't come in this life, and Scripture holds out the promise of a day when there will be no more pain (Revelation 21:4). Waiting isn't a sign that you prayed wrong or didn't believe enough. Keep praying, keep asking others to pray, and keep following your care team's guidance.
“‘My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness.’”
2 Corinthians 12:9
God's answer to Paul's long prayer for relief was grace that was enough for each day.
Frequently asked questions
What is a short prayer for healing?
Jeremiah 17:14 is a short prayer straight from Scripture: "Heal me, O Yahweh, and I will be healed. Save me, and I will be saved; for you are my praise."
How do you pray for someone's healing?
Pray for them by name, ask God to heal and comfort them, pray for wisdom for their doctors and caregivers, and pray for peace and hope for their family. James 5:14–15 encourages praying together, so invite others to join you.
What does the Bible say about praying for healing?
The Bible encourages believers to pray for the sick (James 5:14–15), presents God as a healer (Exodus 15:26, Psalm 103:3), and shows Jesus healing many people (Matthew 9:35). It also honestly records seasons of waiting and weakness (2 Corinthians 12:7–10).
