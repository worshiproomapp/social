A prayer for strength in hard times
By the Worship Room team · November 10, 2026
When you need strength, you can pray: "Lord, I'm weak and tired, but You give power to the weak. Renew my strength for today, and let Your power show up where I'm running out. Amen." Below is a fuller prayer for strength, short prayers for specific situations, and verses to pray.

A prayer for strength
Lord, I'm worn out. I've been trying to hold everything together on my own, and I can't anymore. You promise to give power to the weak and strength to the weary, so here I am, weak and weary. Renew me. Give me strength for the next hour and the next decision. When I want to quit, remind me that You're with me. Let Your strength be made perfect in my weakness. Amen.
Prayers for strength in specific moments
When you're exhausted
Jesus, You invited the weary to come to You. I'm coming. Give me rest where I can find it and strength where I can't.
When caring for someone else
Lord, caring for them is draining me. Fill me up so I can keep loving well, and send people to care for me too.
When you're sick
God, my body feels weak. Be my strength today. Help me rest without guilt and heal in Your time.
When you're facing a hard decision
Father, give me the courage to do what's right, even when it's hard. Steady my heart and make my path clear.
When you're tempted to give up
Lord, I want to quit. Don't let me. Help me take one more step, and then another, with You.
Scripture to pray for strength
“He gives power to the weak. He increases the strength of him who has no might. Even the youths faint and get weary, and the young men utterly fall; but those who wait for Yahweh will renew their strength. They will mount up with wings like eagles. They will run, and not be weary. They will walk, and not faint.”
Isaiah 40:29–31
Pray it: "I'm the weak one You give power to. Renew my strength as I wait for You."
“My flesh and my heart fails, but God is the strength of my heart and my portion forever.”
Psalm 73:26
Pray it: "My heart is failing, but You are the strength of my heart."
“‘My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness.’”
2 Corinthians 12:9
Pray it: "Let Your grace be enough for today."
“Yahweh is my strength and my shield. My heart has trusted in him, and I am helped.”
Psalm 28:7
Pray it: "You are my strength and my shield. I trust You."
Strength isn't the same as pushing through
Sometimes the strongest thing you can do is rest, ask for help, or say no. In Scripture, God's strength often looks like sustaining people through hard seasons rather than removing every hardship. Let others pray for you, and let them carry part of the load.
Frequently asked questions
What is a powerful prayer for strength?
A powerful prayer for strength is an honest one. Tell God how weak you feel and ask Him to renew you, as Isaiah 40:29–31 promises. Praying Scripture, such as Psalm 73:26 or 2 Corinthians 12:9, can give you words when you're too tired to find your own.
What is a short prayer for strength?
"Lord, You give power to the weak. Renew my strength for today. Amen." It's drawn from Isaiah 40:29–31.
What does the Bible say about strength in hard times?
The Bible says God is "our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble" (Psalm 46:1) and that His power is "made perfect in weakness" (2 Corinthians 12:9). Those who wait for the Lord "will renew their strength" (Isaiah 40:31).
