Experiments

  • tt-pace: A 1.5 s hook and quicker reveals (15–20 s videos) hold a bigger share of the video than the standard pace. Metric: average watch % of length; arms standard vs fast; judged at 4+ posts per arm.

  • tt-format: Photo-mode carousels reach as many people as videos on a new account. Metric: reach per post, saves per 100 reach (videos are the comparison group: TikTok posts of kind video); arms video vs photo; judged at 4+ posts per arm.
